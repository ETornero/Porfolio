ClassNotes is a demo of a game in the Platformer genre inspired by the classic game VVVVVV. In it, we take on the role of one of a student's sketches that comes to life and must travel through the pages of their notes to collect pixelated fruits.

Monkey Street is a demo of a Survival game with a Top-Down perspective, inspired by the "Commandos" game series. In this game, we play as a monkey with the ability to shoot bananas. Our mission is to fight against humans who have turned into zombies.
